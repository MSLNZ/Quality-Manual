\section{The GUM}

The \textit{Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement} (GUM)
is the suite of JCGM documents providing guidance on the evaluation and
expression of measurement uncertainty. The original GUM, now designated
GUM Part~3, was published as a stand-alone document. Its current base
edition is JCGM~100:2008, a republication of the 1995 GUM with minor
corrections. Amendment~1, on nonlinearity in measurement models, was
published in 2026. The former Supplements~1 and~2 are now designated GUM
Parts~7 and~8, respectively.

The 2008 base edition is available free of charge from the BIPM:
\begin{quote}
\url{https://doi.org/10.59161/JCGM100-2008E}
\end{quote}

\paragraph{Bibliographic reference}
The following reference is suitable for academic publications:
\begin{quote}
Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology (JCGM) 2008,
\textit{Evaluation of measurement data --- Guide to the expression of
uncertainty in measurement}, JCGM~100:2008, 1st edition.
\end{quote}

\paragraph{GUM reference for calibration reports}
For the purpose of the following reference, GUM refers to GUM Part~3,
JCGM~100:2008.

The following is sufficient:
\begin{quote}
See the \textit{``Guide to the expression of uncertainty in measurement
JCGM 100:2008''} (BIPM, 1st edition, 2008) for an explanation of terms.
\end{quote}

Not all parts of the GUM suite have yet been published. Publication of a
part does not itself constitute its adoption within MSL's Quality System.
The table below records both the publication status of each part and
MSL's current position on its use.

JCGM GUM-7 &
JCGM~101 &
Guide to the expression of uncertainty in measurement -- Part~7: Propagation of distributions using a Monte Carlo method &
Published &
\textbf{Not adopted.}

This is GUM Supplement~1\cite{GUM_S1}. Its use is not recommended because its interpretation of probability in uncertainty statements is Bayesian\cite{WHITE_16}. Consequently, MSL does not consider uncertainty statements obtained using its Monte Carlo method to have a physically meaningful interpretation. \\
\hline

JCGM GUM-8 &
JCGM~102 &
Guide to the expression of uncertainty in measurement -- Part~8: Extension to any number of output quantities &
Published &
\textbf{Partially adopted.}

This is GUM Supplement~2\cite{GUM_S2}. The extensions to the analytical techniques in GUM Part~3 for multidimensional measurands are adopted. The Monte Carlo material is not adopted for the reasons applying to GUM Part~7. A more extensive review of multidimensional extensions to the GUM is available~\cite{HALL_15}\\
